# Demo script — about 60 seconds

No voiceover is required; the motion and sound carry it. If you record with voice, use
the lines in quotes.

| Time | Shot | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Briefing | Land on the page. "Capt. Hale has the case." Click once so the music and voice start; let the three beats play, then **CONTINUE**. |
| 0:09 | Setup | Show the full-screen tip (**F11**), the name input, and **SINGLE PLAYER**. Toss the coin and let it land, then **START THE CASE**. |
| 0:15 | Board | "One detective against the House. New Game is in the top-right panel." |
| 0:19 | Captain | Show the note revealing word by word; pause on the grade. |
| 0:22 | House | Play a move; let the board lock, show **THINKING**, and watch the House answer. |
| 0:28 | Finish | Complete the row; let the yarn draw and the CASE CLOSED label land. |
| 0:34 | Verdict | The captain takes over. Show the winner, the note, and the primary rematch; pause on **RESET SCORES** / **STAY ON THE BOARD**. |
| 0:41 | Match history | Stay, then open **MATCH HISTORY** in the CASE CONTROL panel; show the saved result. |
| 0:47 | New Game | Click it. Show the briefing, setup, mode, and side returning; scores stay. |
| 0:53 | Keyboard | Arrow to a cell, press Enter. Show the focus ring and the turn change. |
| 0:57 | Mobile | Cut to 390px; show the full-width New Game and the scoreboard. |
| 1:01 | Sound | Tap **MUSIC** off and on; point at **SFX** as the captain's voice and coin control. |
